What We Do

Three Pillars of Impact

Every rupee directed by our donors works within one of these carefully separated pools.

Financial Aid

We sponsor higher education degrees and fast-track vocational skills — including 3-month Tally and IT certifications — to prevent student dropouts caused solely by financial pressure.

Trust Marker: Funds disbursed exclusively via cheque to college and institutional counters. Zero cash handling.

Mentorship & Career Guidance

We don't just fund students — we counsel them. Our committee provides hands-on mentorship to help youth choose the right educational streams and navigate early career placements.

✦ Ensuring donor funds are spent on courses with the highest employment probability.

Free Marriage Bureau

Confidential, dignified matchmaking for families. Our volunteer team facilitates introductions with complete respect for privacy.

⚠️ Zakat Compliance: Operated entirely by volunteers. ZERO fees charged. ZERO donation funds used. Fully separated from all Zakat & Sadaqah pools.
